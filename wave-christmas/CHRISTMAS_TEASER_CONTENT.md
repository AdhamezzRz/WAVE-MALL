# The Wave: Christmas teaser, 3 posts

A three-step reveal: a hint, the big moment, then the invitation.
Palette: forest-green velvet, warm gold, cream and cocoa, the same as the posted grid, with no Christmas red.
Format: 1080 × 1350 (4:5). Post 02 is a film. A 4:5 feed version and the 3:4 original are both included.
Arabic captions are drafts and need a native copywriter's review.

---

## 01 · Something is wrapping up (teaser still)
**Visual:** hands with a burgundy manicure tie a forest-green velvet ribbon on a cream gift box. Pine sprigs, dried orange, cinnamon, a brass bell and a candle sit on cocoa linen. It is shot in warm window light with film grain.
**On the post:** SOMETHING *is wrapping up.* / DECEMBER · THE WAVE

**Caption (EN):**
Something is wrapping up.
The Wave is getting ready for its first December. Stay close.

**Caption (AR):**
شيء ما يُغلَّف الآن.
ذا ويف تستعد لأول ديسمبر لها. ابقوا قريبين.

**Goal:** curiosity. Don't reveal anything yet.

## 02 · The Wave is getting wrapped (animated film)
**Visual (9.6 seconds, with sound):** the real building at blue hour, shot from a fixed camera. A giant forest-green velvet ribbon floats down from the sky, wraps the curved facade and ties itself into a huge bow on the roof. Fairy lights then ripple along every curve and the Christmas tree lights up.
**On the film:**
- Opening: THE WAVE IS *getting wrapped.*
- End card: *see you under* THE LIGHTS. · logo · COMING THIS DECEMBER

**Caption (EN):**
The Wave is getting wrapped. 🎀
Our biggest gift to New Cairo is almost ready. See you under the lights this December.

**Caption (AR):**
ذا ويف يتم تغليفها. 🎀
أكبر هدية منا للقاهرة الجديدة أصبحت جاهزة تقريباً. نراكم تحت الأضواء هذا ديسمبر.

**Post as:** a Reel shared to the feed, with `02-reel-cover.jpg` as the cover. The 3:4 version without text is for Stories or ads. Use the film's own sound or a licensed seasonal track.
**Goal:** reach and shares. This is the hero piece of the teaser.

**Visual:** a macro shot of an antique gold mercury-glass bauble on a fir branch. The curved, glowing Wave building is reflected in it, surrounded by warm bokeh.

**Caption (EN):**
Dear December, we've been expecting you.
Lights, gifts and long evenings are coming to The Wave. Turn on notifications so you don't miss the first night.

**Goal:** saves and notifications ahead of the lighting night.
