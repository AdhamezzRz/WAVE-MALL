# The Wave: set 2, posts 07–12

Format 1080 × 1350 (4:5), files in `posts/07…12`. Arabic captions are drafts for native review.

## 07 · The Autumn Edit (fashion, carousel cover)
- **Visual:** magazine cover. The top 70% is a photo of hands carrying a cream shopping bag with green ribbon handles and a green leather bag, walking down travertine steps at golden hour. Below is a cream panel with cocoa type. Small logo top left, "VOL. 01" top right.
- **On the post:** *the* AUTUMN EDIT / CAMEL, CREAM AND A TOUCH OF GREEN. / SWIPE →
- **Caption EN:** The Autumn Edit, Vol. 01. Camel, cream and a touch of green: the colours of the season, and of The Wave. Swipe for the pieces we're loving now.
- **Caption AR:** مختارات الخريف، العدد الأول. درجات الكاميل والكريمي ولمسة من الأخضر، ألوان الموسم وألوان ذا ويف. اسحب لترى القطع المفضلة لدينا.
- **Carousel slides 2–5:** one product or look per slide from a tenant, each tagged with the tenant's handle.
- **Goal:** fashion authority and tenant promotion. Saves and shares.

## 08 · The story behind our mark (brand story)
- **Visual:** a gold-foil close-up of the A–V ligature from the logo, filling the top of a deep forest-green post.
- **On the post:** THE STORY BEHIND OUR MARK / *one stroke,* TWO LETTERS, ONE WAVE. / Where the A meets the V, a single line rises and falls like the sea. It is the moment our name becomes a shape.
- **Caption EN:** One stroke, two letters, one wave. Look closely at our logo: where the A meets the V, a single line rises and falls like the sea. That curve is the whole idea of The Wave, a place that flows.
- **Caption AR:** ضربة واحدة، حرفان، موجة واحدة. انظر عن قرب إلى شعارنا: حيث يلتقي حرف A بحرف V، يرتفع خط واحد وينخفض مثل البحر. هذا المنحنى هو فكرة ذا ويف كلها، مكان ينساب.
- **Reel idea:** a slow camera pan across the gold foil, ending on the full logo.
- **Goal:** make the new identity memorable and give the logo a meaning people can repeat.

## 09 · Colours of The Wave (identity)
- **Visual:** cream background with six arch-shaped swatches (the arch echoes the building's curves), each with its hex code and a poetic name.
- **On the post:** COLOURS OF *The Wave* / Sea Glass #B3DCCF · Cocoa #5A3B22 · Caramel #7A4826 · Desert Sand #BBA286 · Palm Forest #1D3A2E · Gold Hour (foil)
- **Caption EN:** The colours of The Wave. Sea Glass, Cocoa, Caramel, Desert Sand, Palm Forest and Gold Hour: six shades taken from the sea, the desert and the light of New Cairo. Which one is yours?
- **Caption AR:** ألوان ذا ويف. زجاج البحر، الكاكاو، الكراميل، رمال الصحراء، غابة النخيل، والساعة الذهبية: ستة ألوان مستوحاة من البحر والصحراء وضوء القاهرة الجديدة. أي لون يشبهك؟
- **Goal:** comments and identity recall. The palette works as a brand guide for tenants and creators too.

## 10 · A day at The Wave (lifestyle itinerary)
- **Visual:** warm sand background with a typographic timeline: times in italic serif, activities in capitals, separated by fine cocoa rules.
- **On the post:** *a day at* THE WAVE / 10:00 COFFEE ON THE TERRACE · 12:30 THE STYLE EDIT · 17:00 GOLDEN HOUR ON THE STEPS · 20:30 DINNER UNDER THE LIGHTS / SAVE THIS FOR YOUR WEEKEND
- **Caption EN:** A day at The Wave. Coffee on the terrace at ten, the style edit at half past twelve, golden hour on the steps at five, dinner under the lights at half past eight. Save this for your weekend.
- **Caption AR:** يوم في ذا ويف. قهوة على التراس في العاشرة، جولة تسوق في الثانية عشرة والنصف، الساعة الذهبية على الدرج في الخامسة، وعشاء تحت الأضواء في الثامنة والنصف. احفظ المنشور لعطلة نهاية الأسبوع.
- **Story idea:** four story frames, one per time slot, each tagging a real tenant.
- **Note:** the times are illustrative. Check them against real opening hours before posting.
- **Goal:** saves and planned visits.

## 11 · After dark (architecture at night)
- **Visual:** the building's curved ribbon balconies traced by warm LED lines against a deep night sky, with blurred figures on the wet plaza. No signage.
- **On the post:** AFTER DARK, *the wave glows.*
- **Caption EN:** After dark, The Wave glows. The curves turn into lines of light, and the evening begins.
- **Caption AR:** بعد الغروب، تتوهج ذا ويف. تتحول المنحنيات إلى خطوط من الضوء، وتبدأ الأمسية.
- **Reel idea:** a time-lapse of the LED lines switching on at dusk.
- **Goal:** position The Wave as an evening destination for dining and nightlife.

## 12 · Which wave are you? (engagement)
- **Visual:** forest-green stripes with a cream arch-shaped card holding four lettered options.
- **On the post:** WHICH WAVE *are you?* / A · THE MORNING WAVE, *coffee and calm* / B · THE STYLE WAVE, *new season, new looks* / C · THE GOLDEN WAVE, *sunset on the terrace* / D · THE NIGHT WAVE, *dinner and city lights* / TELL US YOUR LETTER IN THE COMMENTS
- **Caption EN:** Which wave are you? A, B, C or D? Tell us your letter in the comments and tag the friend who shares your wave.
- **Caption AR:** أي موجة أنت؟ أ: موجة الصباح، قهوة وهدوء. ب: موجة الأناقة، موسم جديد وإطلالات جديدة. ج: الموجة الذهبية، غروب على التراس. د: موجة الليل، عشاء وأضواء المدينة. اكتب حرفك في التعليقات وشارك صديقاً يشبهك.
- **Follow-up:** reply to comments with a matching tenant recommendation, and run a story poll with the same four options.
- **Goal:** comments, tags and reach.
