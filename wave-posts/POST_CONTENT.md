Format: 1080 × 1350 (4:5), files in `posts/`. Grid preview: `posts-grid-preview.jpg`.
Brand line on every post: **More than a destination.**
Palette, taken from your artboards and logo: mint `#B3DCCF`, cocoa `#5A3B22`, caramel `#7A4826`, sand `#BBA286`, forest `#1D3A2E`, cream `#F1E9DB` and gold foil.
Type: a clean grotesque for capitals (Inter Display), a calligraphic script for the soft line (Pinyon Script), and the logo itself as the serif.

## Posting notes
- **Grid order:** Instagram shows the newest post first. To make the profile grid read 01→06 as in the preview, publish 06 first and 01 last. If the launch story matters more than the grid, post 01 first.
- **Cadence:** every other day over two weeks, alternating a design post (01, 03, 06) with a photo post (02, 04, 05).
- **Handle:** end captions with "Follow @thewavemall".

## About the visuals
- Posts 02, 04 and 05 use AI photography generated with Higgsfield. Post 06 uses an AI-generated green paper texture with the gold foil applied to your logo here. Posts 01 and 03 are pure design.
- Post 04 is an AI image based on your photo of the building, with no signage. It shows the building's look, not an exact photo of it.
- The fashion and café photos are illustrative. For paid campaigns, consider a real shoot with the actual tenants.
