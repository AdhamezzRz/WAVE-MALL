# "The WAVE" sign, recreated as clean vector

Built from your photo/reference of the lit sign: a small bold rounded "The", then WAVE drawn as
neon-tube strokes (W = two waves, A = a hump with its own slanted left leg, V flowing into the
top bar of the E, E = three bars). Each tube is one smooth round-capped stroke whose coordinates
were measured from `reference-clean.png` and checked by overlaying on it.

- `build_logo.py` writes every SVG (`python3 build_logo.py`); tweak stroke widths/points at the top.
- 01 neon look (glow on black) · 02 gold/black · 03 black/ivory · 04 forest/ivory · 05 ivory/emerald
- 06-08 transparent (black, ivory, gold). PNG previews rendered at 2400 px.

A visual reconstruction from a low-resolution photo, not the official artwork. If the owner has
the original files, use those and have a designer compare.
