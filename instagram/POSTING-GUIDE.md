# The Wave — Instagram launch kit

**One line. Six posts.** One continuous hairline wave runs through the whole grid, the same single stroke that joins the A and the V in the logo. The top row is one night panorama split across three posts, with the rooftop sign in the centre. Each post also stands alone when opened.

## Upload order (important)

Instagram puts the newest post top-left, so upload in this order, one post at a time, and pick the **3:4** crop (the original size) when posting:

1. `post-1-leasing.jpg` (bottom right)
2. `post-2-the-mark.jpg` (bottom middle)
3. `post-3-more-than-a-destination.jpg` (bottom left)
4. `post-4-panorama-right.jpg` (top right)
5. `post-5-panorama-sign.jpg` (top middle)
6. `post-6-panorama-left.jpg` (top left)

Posting all six in one session keeps the panorama and the wave line together. Don't pin posts until all six are up.
