# The Wave · Instagram grid v4 (12 posts, with the opening panorama)

The feed opens with one picture split across three posts: the real Wave at golden hour, edge to edge. The building and the rooftop THE WAVE sign are from our photograph of the mall, with the sky set to the brand mint.

- One fine line enters from the left, passes behind the rooftop sign and ends at a small caption in the sky: EVERY LINE / LEADS SOMEWHERE.
- A version with no text and no line is in the `silent` folder.

Above the panorama sit the nine single posts, as a 3×3 grid.

**Post in order 1 → 12.** The panorama is posts 1–3. Publish them a few minutes apart, right part first (`post-01`), then the middle (`post-02`), then the left (`post-03`). The panorama only lines up when the number of posts after it is a multiple of three, which is why there are 9 single posts above it.

## Upload settings

- Posts 1–3 are **1080 × 1440 (3:4)**. In the cropping step choose **3:4** (or "original"), not 4:5 or 1:1, so the three parts meet exactly on the profile.
- Your own 8 posts and post 4 are 1080 × 1350 (4:5). The profile grid shows them cropped to 3:4.
- Don't add filters or edits in the app: the line and the type have to meet across the posts.
