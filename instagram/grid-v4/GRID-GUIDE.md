**Post in order 1 → 12.** The panorama is posts 1–3. Publish them a few minutes apart, right part first (`post-01-panorama-right`), then the middle (`post-02-panorama-middle`), then the left (`post-03-panorama-left`). The panorama only lines up when the number of posts after it is a multiple of three, which is why there are 9 single posts above it.

## Upload settings

- Posts 1–3 are **1080 × 1440 (3:4)**. In the cropping step choose **3:4** (or "original"), not 4:5 or 1:1, so the three parts meet exactly on the profile.
- Your own 8 posts and post 4 are 1080 × 1350 (4:5). The profile grid shows them cropped to 3:4.
- Don't add filters or edits in the app: the three panorama posts have to meet exactly.
